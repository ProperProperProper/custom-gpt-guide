# Common mistakes

Worth heading off before your friend hits them.

**Treating Instructions like a personality bio.**
"Be witty and knowledgeable" gives the model nothing to actually follow.
Behavior over adjectives — say what it *does*, not what it's like.

**Uploading everything at once and hoping.**
A pile of unrelated PDFs makes retrieval worse, not better. Curate what
actually belongs to the job before uploading it.

**Skipping the adversarial test.**
A GPT that only gets tried with polite, on-topic questions will surprise you
the first time it doesn't. Test it like someone trying to confuse it, not
just someone using it correctly.

**Calling it "trained."**
Say "configured" or "set up" with your friend instead. It keeps expectations
accurate for what happens later if they want real fine-tuning instead of a
Custom GPT — a genuinely different (and much heavier) process.
